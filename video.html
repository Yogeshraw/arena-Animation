About Video EDITING

Learn every skill you need to edit professional video — from your first cut to final delivery. This course covers colour grading, audio mixing, motion graphics, VFX, and the business side of freelance editing. Whether you are starting from zero or levelling up, you will build real skills on real projects.

What you will learn

This course takes you through the complete video editing process, starting with NLE fundamentals and timeline mechanics, then moving into advanced colour grading, audio design, motion graphics, and visual effects. You will develop editorial judgment to shape compelling stories from raw footage, not just by pushing technical buttons. You will also learn how to work with clients, price your services, and deliver polished projects across every major platform. AI-powered tools, short-form social editing, and collaborative workflows are all covered. By the end, you will have a professional demo reel and the skills to back it up.
