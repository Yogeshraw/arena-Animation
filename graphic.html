About Graphic Design

Turn your creative instincts into a professional design career with a curriculum that covers everything from colour theory and typography to brand identity and digital UI. You will master industry-standard tools, build real-world projects, and graduate with a polished portfolio that helps you secure a position. This is the complete graphic design education — built for people who are serious about making it.

What you will learn

This course takes you through every core discipline a working graphic designer needs to master. You will learn foundational design principles, colour theory, typography, and how to use professional software, including vector, raster, and layout applications. You will design logos, brand identity systems, print collateral, and digital UI components. Supplementary modules cover motion graphics, illustration, AI tools, and client management. By the end, you will have a complete portfolio and the business skills to launch your career or freelance practice.
